Add Product In 3 Steps

  1. Upload product images to a public URL such as Google Drive, Netlify, or a CDN.
  2. Keep the Sheet columns in the current A-Q order so every new row maps into the storefront automatically.
  3. Refresh the catalogue page and the new item appears in its collection automatically.
