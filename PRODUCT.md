# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Clients exploring a bespoke Maris ring, often on mobile or desktop before speaking with the atelier. Their primary job is to shape a clear, private design brief and request a consultation without mistaking the experience for a confirmed purchase or technically exact CAD preview.

## Product Purpose

Help clients make coherent ring choices, preserve the draft safely, and hand a complete, truthful brief to the Maris team. Success means the client understands which preferences were captured, Maris receives the same information, and both sides can continue through a human consultation.

## Brand Personality

Premium, calm, truthful. The interface should feel like a private atelier tool: considered and reassuring, with enough visual richness to inspire confidence without overstating technical precision.

## Anti-references

- Generic ecommerce configurators that imply checkout, guaranteed availability, or instant production.
- Toy-like 3D demos whose controls do not visibly affect the product.
- Dense CAD software language or controls that make a consultation brief feel technical and intimidating.
- Artificial urgency, fake scarcity, and unsupported claims about price or fulfilment.

## Design Principles

1. Make every control visibly honest about what it changes.
2. Keep the primary task focused on building and reviewing a consultation brief.
3. Preserve client choices across navigation and sign-in without surprising data loss.
4. Use premium restraint: clear hierarchy, calm feedback, and purposeful motion.
5. Keep the browser, API, email, database, and admin representation of a design consistent.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Use semantic controls, explicit labels, visible focus, keyboard-operable steps, meaningful status messages, reduced-motion support, sufficient contrast, and responsive layouts without horizontal page overflow.
